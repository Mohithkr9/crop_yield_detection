About AgriPredict

AgriPredict is a machine learning based crop yield prediction system.

The system uses agricultural information such as crop type, area, year, rainfall, pesticide usage and average temperature to predict crop yield.

🌾 Supported Crops

🌽 Maize

A major cereal crop used for food and animal feed.

🍌 Plantains

A tropical crop commonly used as a staple food.

🥔 Potatoes

An important food crop grown in many regions.

🌾 Rice

One of the major staple food crops around the world.

🌾 Sorghum

A cereal crop that can grow in relatively dry areas.

🌱 Soybeans

Used for food, animal feed and oil production.

🍠 Sweet Potatoes

A nutritious root crop grown in many regions.

🌾 Wheat

An important cereal crop used for many food products.

🌱 Yams

A tropical root crop used as an important food source.

🤖 Machine Learning

AgriPredict uses a Linear Regression machine learning model trained using historical agricultural data.
